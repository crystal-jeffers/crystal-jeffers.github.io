Employee Lifecycle System

Built, pilot pending

Communication · Efficiency

A complete management interface designed to automate routine steps, track case progress, and support faster turnaround for every new hire, role change and departure.

Open the guide site ↗ How it works ↗
Challenge, build & business impact

The challenge

Bringing on a new hire involves IT, Safety, Fleet, Facilities, Accounting and often the union hall. Each manager carried those steps in their own way, which made progress hard to see during a busy week. Role changes and departures raised the same coordination needs, with added care for confidentiality.

What I built

  • One engine, three case typesOnboarding, role change and offboarding each start with one form and build their own checklist from a shared step library, filtered by role, location, union, vehicle and more.
  • One owner per stepEvery hand-off is split into a request step and a confirmation step, so nothing stalls between teams.
  • Automation with judgmentManagers choose whether routine emails send for them or wait as drafts. Sensitive cases, like involuntary exits, always wait for a person.
  • Docs for each audienceA guide site with separate paths for HR and managers, a timeline, an FAQ keyed to every email, and plain-language definitions.

Business impact

Supports the goal of enhancing onboarding and ongoing support as new hires join the division and learn the NorCal Way. Every hire, role change and departure follows the same steps, whichever manager owns it, which creates consistent workflows and smoother hand-offs between HR, IT, Safety, Fleet and Accounting. Because it was built from the superintendents' own New Hire Protocols, it reflects a process they already know.

  • Human-in-the-loop
  • Role-based UX

Start-Up Crew Tracking & Dispatch

Built, pilot pending

Communication · Access

A shared scheduling board for the Start-Up crew that tracks every job and accounts for each technician's union holidays, so scheduling stays consistent as the crew grows.

Supervisor guide ↗
Challenge, build & business impact

The challenge

Start-up crew schedules and job status were tracked in a spreadsheet. Union holidays vary by local and by agreement, so checking a technician's availability meant cross-referencing by hand.

What I built

  • Timeline and calendar viewsEvery technician's jobs, training and time off in one place, with job tracking and a start-up checklist per job.
  • Union holidays added automaticallyEach tech gets their own local's paid holidays and off days, about 90 days ahead.
  • Warn, don't blockIf a job lands on someone's holiday, the bar is flagged and the warning clears when it moves. The supervisor keeps the final call.

Business impact

Brings consistency and predictability to crew scheduling, and gives the department room to take on more work as it grows. Union holiday rules appear right where scheduling decisions are made: at launch, 132 entries for 16 union technicians, with new crew members added automatically.

  • Spreadsheet-to-database migration
  • Rules modeling
  • Scheduling UX
  • Exception handling

Start-Up Procedures Site

Live

Access

Start-up procedures reorganized into a phone-friendly site, with a focused view for each role so technicians can find their steps right from the field.

Open the site ↗
Challenge, build & business impact

The challenge

The start-up procedures lived in a thorough 12-page document written for every role at once. On a job site, on a phone, finding the few steps that applied to you took time.

What I built

  • Role-based viewsApprentices, technicians, lead technicians and supervisors each see their own procedures first.
  • Built to be scanned on siteLarge text that respects phone settings, collapsible sections, jump-to navigation and chunked checklists.
  • Maintainable without a developerProcedures are plain text files, and one settings file controls which roles see what.

Business impact

Helps people understand what's expected of them to succeed in their role, right from the field. New hires learn the same procedures from day one, and the team can keep the content current without outside help.

  • Content design
  • Mobile-first
  • Accessibility

Technician PM Reporting

In use

Communication · Efficiency

Personalized monthly emails that give each technician a clear view of their own preventive maintenance work, organized by next step.

Challenge, build & business impact

The challenge

Each technician's preventive maintenance (PM) work sat within the department's full schedule, so check-ins began with finding the right items before discussing next steps.

What I built

  • A monthly schedule email per technicianOnly their outstanding and upcoming PMs. Sales and backup technicians are copied, so everyone covering the account stays informed.
  • A personalized action emailOpen items broken into tables by what they need: notes needed, return visit needed, or ready to schedule.

Business impact

Strengthens collaboration between technicians, sales and backup coverage, so customer commitments stay on track. Check-ins focus on decisions, supporting reliable service and lasting customer relationships.

PM Parts Ordering with Technician Review

In use

Efficiency · Communication

Automated parts ordering with a technician review built in, so each order is checked by the person who knows the equipment before it's placed.

Challenge, build & business impact

The challenge

PM parts lists changed often, and keeping every version current took constant attention. When one customer had several orders in a month, telling the orders apart took extra effort.

What I built

  • Automatic ordering with Excel macrosEvery order gets its own correct PO number, even when one customer has several orders in a month.
  • A review step before orderingEach technician gets the upcoming parts lists for their sites to check and update before the order goes out.

Business impact

Reflects a commitment to excellence, with no shortcuts on what goes to a customer's site. The technician who knows the equipment reviews each order, and clear PO numbers make every order easy to trace.

  • VBA / Excel macros
  • Quality control design
  • Human-in-the-loop
